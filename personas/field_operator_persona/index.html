Field Operator Persona

User Persona: Alex Smith, Field Operator Level 2

Profile - Name: Alex Smith - Age: 32 - Occupation: Field Operator Level 2 - Experience: 8 years in field operations, specializing in equipment maintenance and troubleshooting. - Comfort Level: High with legacy DCS/SCADA systems, Moderate with new digital platforms, and Low with advanced analytics tools.

Day To Day Responsibilities

  1. Monitors the physical processes via HMI/SCADA systems
  2. Acknowledges, diagnoses, and responds to process alarms
  3. Walks the plant floor to physically inspect assets
  4. Coordinates with maintenance for hardware replacement or repair when necessary
  5. Executes safe shift handovers by reviewing the maintenance logs and Master Alarm Database.

Goals

  1. To Keep the plant running safely and efficiently.
  2. He Wants absolute certainty that any automated action won’t override hardcoded safety interlocks (SIS) or endanger personnel on the floor.
  3. He Wants to know WHY the system is failing before it actually breaks.
  4. He Needs the AI to act like a knowledgeable assistant, not an autonomous boss. He wants to see the “math” or reasoning behind any recommendation.

Pain Points

  1. Alarm Fatigue: Alex is often bombarded with alarms, many of which are false positives or low-priority. This can lead to alarm fatigue, making it difficult to identify critical issues.
  2. Lack of Explanation: Alex hates that the system tells him that something is failing but not the why behind it.
  3. Loss of Human Oversight: Alex is concerned about the
  4. Loss of Control: Alex fears that AI systems taking autonomous control of critical infrastructure without human oversight or approval could cause catastrophic failures.
  5. Time-Sync Jitter: Alex gets easily annoying by false-positive alarms.

How Marcus Interacts with Oversight ICS

  1. The Human-in-the-Loop: Marcus monitors the system via the dashboard. When Oversight’s Bytewax stream processor detects a Z-score drift (e.g., a pump is about to fail), the LangGraph Agent proposes a failover. Marcus receives this via the Grey Scale Dashboard. He relies on the system’s Pessimistic Confirmation. The system won’t act without his JWT-authenticated approval.
  2. Dual-Authorization: If a catastrophic failure requires a high-impact override, Marcus knows he must get secondary approval. He uses his Primary Operator JWT, but relies on a Supervisor to provide the Secondary Authorization to unlock the system.
  3. Root Cause Analysis (RAG): Instead of guessing what went wrong, Marcus reads the AI’s generated reasoning trace. Because the AI is grounded via pgVector and RAG, it provides Marcus with the exact technical manual excerpt and intent justification hash for the proposed command.
  4. HMI Visual Cues: Marcus is trained to look for Oversight’s specific HMI states. If he attempts a manual override that the AI deems unsafe, he expects to hear the “Single Beep-Low tone” auditory feedback and see the Yellow Banner + Timer (Safety Delay Active) enforcing a throttle on his commands to prevent panicked, unsafe actions.

Quote: “I don’t need a computer to run the plant for me. I need it to tell me exactly what’s failing, why it’s failing, and give me a safe plan to fix it before things go critical. If I can’t audit its logic, I’m not hitting approve.”
